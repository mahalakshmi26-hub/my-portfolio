What This Tool Does

Open Graph Checker reads the og: and twitter: meta tags on any page and shows you exactly what a shared link will look like on eight different platforms — before you find out the hard way that the preview card is blank, the wrong image got picked, or the crawler couldn't even reach the page. It's built for anyone who publishes pages and cares how they look when someone shares the link — bloggers, marketers, and SEO folks who don't want to guess.

Key Features

👀
8-Platform Previews
Live mockups for Twitter/X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, Telegram and Google Search — each image cropped to that platform's real aspect ratio.
🖼️
Image Size & Crop Safety
Downloads the actual og:image and checks its real dimensions, file size and aspect ratio — not just whether the tag exists.
🐦
Twitter Card Cross-Check
Compares twitter:card tags against their Open Graph fallbacks, so you know exactly what X will actually show.
🤖
Robots.txt Crawler Check
Tests robots.txt against the real Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord and Telegram crawler user-agents — catches the invisible reason a preview silently fails.
💯
OG Score, With Fixes
Every page gets a 0-100 score and a specific, actionable fix for each issue found — not just a pass/fail.
📋
Bulk Check, Up To 20 URLs
Paste a whole list of pages and get a scored table plus a CSV export in one pass.

How to Use It

1
Paste a URL
Drop any page URL into the Single URL tab, or switch to Bulk Check to test up to 20 URLs at once.
2
Review the score and previews
See your OG score, exactly how the card looks on all 8 platforms, and a plain-English list of issues with fixes.
3
Fix the flagged tags and re-check
Update the tags on your page, then run the check again to confirm the score improved before you share the link.

Why I Recommend It

I built this after one too many times finding out a page's link preview looked broken only after someone had already shared it. Most free OG checkers stop at "the tag exists" — this one actually downloads the image to check its real dimensions, tests robots.txt against the real crawler user-agents, and shows you the image crop for each platform separately, since Facebook, Twitter and WhatsApp don't all crop it the same way.

  • I run new landing pages through it before sharing them anywhere
  • The robots.txt crawler check has caught blocked-bot issues that no other free tool surfaced
  • Bulk mode makes it fast to sanity-check a whole batch of pages at once
