What This Tool Does

Broken links quietly wreck user experience and crawl budget, and most site owners don't find out until Search Console flags 404s weeks later. This tool scans your pages for every outgoing link — internal and external — and checks each one live, so you can catch dead pages, broken redirects, and unreachable external sites before they hurt your rankings.

Key Features

🕸️
Auto-Crawl or Paste URLs
Point it at a homepage and it finds pages via sitemap.xml (or crawls internal links if there's no sitemap) — or paste your own list of page URLs to scan.
🌐
Internal + External Checks
Every link on every scanned page is checked, whether it points within your site or out to another domain.
📊
Link Health Score
A dashboard view with a health ring, status-code breakdown chart, and internal-vs-external split so you can gauge site health at a glance.
⬇️
CSV Export
Download the full report — URL, status, redirects, response time, and which page each broken link was found on — for your audit records.

How to Use It

1
Choose Crawl or Paste
Enter your homepage URL to auto-discover pages, or switch to "Paste URLs" and drop in a specific list of pages to scan.
2
Run the Scan
Click "Check Links." The tool pulls every page, extracts all links, and checks each one — this can take up to a minute for larger sites.
3
Review and Export
Filter results by Broken, Redirects, or Working, see which page each link was found on, and export the full report as CSV.

Why I Built It

In every technical SEO audit I run, broken links come up — and most free checkers only handle one page at a time or hide results behind a paywall past a handful of URLs. I wanted something I could point at a client's site, get a real health score, and hand off a clean CSV without paying for a crawler subscription.

  • I use it before every content migration to catch links that will break when URLs change
  • The sitemap-first crawl means it finds the pages that actually matter to search engines, not just whatever's linked from the homepage
  • Exporting to CSV makes it easy to hand a prioritized fix-list to a dev team
